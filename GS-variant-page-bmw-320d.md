## ⚠️ BMW 320d Timing Chain Failure (F30/F31, N47)
If your 320d is a pre-2015 model with the N47 diesel engine — that's the early F30/F31 generation — timing chain failure due to chain guide wear is a well-documented pattern, subject to NHTSA recall 18V-755. We replace timing chains, guides, and tensioners as standard on every N47 rebuild.
The later F30/F31 (2015-onwards) and G20/G21 generations use a different engine, the B47, which is significantly more reliable and doesn't share this specific issue.

## About the BMW 320d Engine
The 320d has been a mainstay of the 3 Series diesel range since 2012, spanning two generations under one badge. Early F30/F31 models from 2012 to 2015 use the N47, a 2.0-litre diesel that's part of a family known for a specific timing chain issue. From late 2015 onwards, across both the F30/F31 and later the G20/G21, the 320d switched to the B47, a newer, significantly more reliable unit that doesn't share the N47's timing chain concerns. Knowing which one your car has matters, because the components and the known wear pattern differ — which is exactly why we confirm your exact engine by registration before quoting.
The 320d is no longer in production, but we continue to rebuild and replace engines for every one still on the road.
## BMW 320d Engine Codes
### N47 (F30/F31, 2012–2015)
A 2.0-litre diesel fitted to the early F30/F31 320d. Known for timing chain failure due to chain guide wear — see the callout above. We replace timing chains, guides, and tensioners as standard on every N47 rebuild.

### B47 (F30/F31, 2015–2019 / G20/G21, 2019–2024)

## BMW 320d Engine Compatibility
Because the 320d spans two distinct engines across two generations, fitting the wrong replacement — or assuming an N47 part will work on a B47 car — risks a compatibility problem that's expensive to unpick. Before we quote or supply anything, we confirm:
