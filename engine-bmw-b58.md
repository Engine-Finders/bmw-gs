## About the BMW B58 Engine

The B58 is BMW's modular turbocharged inline-six petrol engine, in production since 2015. It's one of the most widely-deployed engines in the current BMW range, fitted across every mainstream model line from the 2 Series up to the flagship 8 Series and X7, plus the new XM and the Z4 roadster. Beyond BMW itself, the same engine family (in modified form) is also used by Toyota in the GR Supra — a detail worth knowing if you're researching this engine's reputation more broadly, though our work here covers BMW-badged applications specifically.

## BMW B58 Applications by Model

Across the **3 Series**, the B58 has powered the 340i (F30/F31, 2015–2019) and continues in the current M340i xDrive (G20/G21). The **4 Series** follows the same pattern with the 440i (F32/F33/F36) and current M440i xDrive. The **5 Series** carries it in the 540i across three generations, including the current PHEV-badged 550e xDrive. The **6 Series** used it in the 640i during its final F06/F12/F13 generation. Both the **7 Series** and **8 Series** use it as their six-cylinder petrol option, in the 730i/740i and 840i respectively. Across the SUV range, the B58 appears in the **X3** M40i and current M50 xDrive, the **X4** M40i, and as the six-cylinder option throughout **X5**, **X6** and **X7**, including their PHEV-badged 50e variants. It's also the entry-level combustion engine in the new **XM 50e**, and powers the current **Z4** M40i.

## BMW B58 Engine Compatibility

Because the B58 spans 14 different models and multiple generations — some in standard petrol form, others as mild-hybrid or plug-in hybrid applications — compatibility comes down to confirming your exact model, generation, and drivetrain spec, which we do by registration. A B58 from a 2 Series isn't a drop-in match for a B58 from an X7, even though it's the same base engine code — ancillaries, mounting, and hybrid-system integration all differ. Before we quote or supply anything, we confirm your exact vehicle.
