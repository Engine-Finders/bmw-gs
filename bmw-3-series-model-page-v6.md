## Why We Rebuild Instead of Swap

Most engine suppliers sell you a used engine of unknown history and hope it matches. We do it differently.

We rebuild the engine your car already has — strip it down, pressure-test it, and replace every worn or damaged component with new OEM parts: timing chains, tensioners, guides, bearings, gaskets and seals. You get a near-new engine that's better than what came out, with a 12-month unlimited-mileage warranty — and you keep your car's original identity.

Used engines carry hidden wear, unknown mileage and compatibility risk. A rebuild removes that risk entirely — and more often than not, it works out cheaper than sourcing and fitting a used replacement, both short and long term.
